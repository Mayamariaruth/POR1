Auction House

Homepage of the Auction House platform.

Live Site GitHub README

Auction House is an auction platform designed for students at Noroff. Registered users can securely create, manage, and participate in auctions through a modern, interactive, and responsive interface. The platform emphasizes transparency, ease of use, and community engagement, allowing users to showcase listings, track bids, and manage profiles with full control.

Key Features

  • Create: Easily create auction listings with title, description, deadline, and image gallery.
  • Manage: Edit or delete your own listings anytime.
  • Bidding: Place bids on other users’ listings with a live history for transparency.
  • Credits: Track available credits and see your balance update in real-time.
  • Profile: Customize profile with bio, avatar, and banner. View listings and bidding history.
  • Search: Browse and search listings by title, content, or tags.
